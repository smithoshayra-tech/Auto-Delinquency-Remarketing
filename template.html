Auto Delinquency Monitor

The share of auto balances 90+ days late is at a record, but new serious delinquencies are still below 2009. The pressure shows up in how many early-late loans roll to 90+ and how long they stay there. Public data through .

Proxy for early-stage collections effectiveness. Dashed line is the 2007-11 peak.

Read before using

  • Industry-wide aggregates from the NY Fed household credit panel, not any one lender's book.
  • Roll rate and persistence are ratios of aggregate flows, not tracked cohorts.
  • Used-vehicle CPI is a consumer price index, not auction recovery value.
  • No public-data model beat "same as now" at forecasting inflow four quarters ahead, so this is a monitor, not a forecast.

Sources: NY Fed Household Debt and Credit Report 2026Q2; FRED. Analysis by Sharon Smith. Full code, 15 data checks and brief are in the project repository.
